# Team Documents

This context defines the shared language for documents owned and edited by a team.

## Language

**Document**:
A team-owned collaborative artifact comprising a title and a rich-text body.
_Avoid_: Doc, file, page

**Team Member**:
A person who belongs to the team that owns a Document. Every Team Member currently has permission to edit the team's Documents.
_Avoid_: Collaborator, viewer

**Editor**:
A Team Member who is actively viewing or changing a Document. An Editor may continue making local changes during a temporary disconnection and synchronize them after reconnecting.
_Avoid_: Author, writer

**Presence**:
The transient identity, cursor or selection, and connection state of an online Editor in a Document.
_Avoid_: Activity, history
